SOBRE NOVA

ARRAIGADOS A NUESTRA TRADICIÓN DE LEVANTAR TROFEOS E INSPIRADOS POR NUESTRA PASIÓN POR LA VICTORIA.

SOMOS NOVA

Entertainment & Sports es una empresa conjunta global de deportes electrónicos que posee y opera el exitoso equipo NOVA League of Legends (LoL) Champions Korea (LCK), junto con equipos en segmentos de juegos competitivos que incluyen Valorant, Rocket League Y COUNTER-STRIKE 2. Aprovechando el rico legado de SKT NOVA, NOVA expandirá sus equipos a nivel mundial, celebrará nuevas victorias y creará aún más oportunidades para que los fanáticos adopten la cultura de juego, el contenido y los jugadores de NOVA en todos los rincones del mundo.

our leader:

JOE MARSH - CEO

Joe Marsh is CEO of NOVA Entertainment & Sports, a global esports joint venture created by Comcast Spectacor and SK Telecom in October 2019. He oversees operations based in Seoul, Philadelphia, Los Angeles and is responsible for the growth and optimization of NOVA’s global presence and expansion into new businesses and revenue streams surrounding competitive gaming culture and lifestyle, content, and merchandise. Joe previously served as Chief Business Officer of Comcast’s Spectacor Gaming division and the Philadelphia Fusion esports franchise that competes in the Overwatch League. Joe was named Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of the Fusion in 2018, providing direct oversight of financials for the team, including budgeting, forecasting, and long-range planning. Prior to joining Spectacor Gaming and the Philadelphia Fusion, Joe held several finance positions at Comcast Spectacor, where he was responsible for the development, coordination, consolidation and communication of the company's annual budgeting, long-range planning and forecasting functions for Comcast Spectacor’s core businesses. Joe graduated with a degree in Marketing from Millersville University and later earned his MBA in Finance, Analytics and Strategic Management from Villanova University. Marsh currently serves as a strategic advisor to Spectacor Gaming, G4tv and the Philadelphia Fusion. Joe previously served as a board member of Nerd Street Gamers. Joe lives in the Philadelphia area with his wife Kelly and daughters Caden and Colbie. ‍
